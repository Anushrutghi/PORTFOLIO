Anushrut’s Theory of Total Energy Recirculation

A theoretical energy-systems framework that views apparent energy loss as unrecovered transformed energy inside a system.

Paper Description

This theory introduces recirculation efficiency as a complementary method for evaluating energy systems. Instead of treating energy loss as waste, it studies whether transformed energy can be recovered, redirected, reused, or reintegrated into the system.
